# Boarding pass (new)

**Fits:** travel, hotels, flights, delivery, tickets, events, cinema.
**Reference:** none yet. The first product built with it becomes the reference: add its folder and tag to `README.md`.

## Signature

- **Header:** a "gate" label on the left ("GATE 03 · BOARDING"); on the right, a small ticket stub with "SEAT 03 / 15" and a perforated edge.
- **Headline:** Bold; the key word in the accent with a small plane/route arc drawn over it (dashed arc, dot at each end).
- **Devices:** clean iPhones with a soft shadow, tilted slightly as if lying on a desk.
- **Motifs:** boarding-pass cards (two parts joined by a dashed perforation with half-circle notches), barcodes and QR blocks, airport-style departure boards (rows of flip tiles), luggage tags with a string, passport stamps (rotated, outlined, 60% opacity), route lines between city codes.

## Slide notes

- Numbers: a departure board: each row is a stat ("SCREENS 84 · ON TIME").
- Features: boarding passes: feature name as the destination, benefit as the class, the screen crop on the stub.
- Flow slides: phones along a route line with "stops" as city-code chips.
- Plans or checkout: a full boarding pass with the price on the stub.
- One dark slide: a night departure board.

## Avoid

Hearts, vinyls, handwritten notes.
