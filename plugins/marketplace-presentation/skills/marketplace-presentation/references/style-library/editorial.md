## Signature

- **Header:** a small tag ("Modules") on the left, then "/03" index, then a 1 px hairline running to the right margin.
- **Headline:** left aligned, Medium weight (not the heaviest), with one *italic* key word in the accent (use the family's Italic style if it has one).
- **Devices:** metallic iPhones tinted with the kit's primary scale (gradient frame), small Dynamic Island, glass sheen.
- **Motifs:** big soft accent circles behind devices; cards with 1 px borders and 24–40 px radius; staircase (rising) phone layouts; coverflow rows; a tilted "screen wall" for all screens; a horizon line of accent colour on the cover.

## Slide notes

- Numbers: a dark band with 6 stats in a row (can be the one dark slide).
- Features: 2×4 bordered cards with a small peeking phone crop.
- Flow slides: one centre phone with connector call-outs, or a rising staircase of 3 phones.
- Marketplace or catalogue flows: coverflow of 5 phones.

## Avoid

All caps, hard shadows, centred layouts on every slide.
