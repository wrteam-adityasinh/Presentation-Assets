# Prompt → response

**Fits:** AI apps, chat, assistants, productivity, customer support.
**Reference:** Aurenix, folder `aurenix/`, tag `aurenix-v1.0.1`.

## Signature

- **Header:** a `/command` chip on the left (`/voice`, `/projects`); a "MSG 03 / 15" chip on the right.
- **Opening on every slide:** a small "YOU ASKED" label, a dark prompt bubble with a customer question ("Can I just talk to it?"), then a reply row: the kit's logo, the product name and three typing dots.
- **Headline:** the "answer": heaviest weight, key words in the accent, followed by a blinking-cursor bar in the accent.
- **Devices:** light silver/titanium iPhones, which make dark app screens pop on a light page.
- **Motifs:** sparkle marks, waveforms, typing dots, chat-thread connectors, composer bars with a send button, slash-command palettes.

## Slide notes

- Features: each feature is a short Q&A pair (question bubble + answer card).
- Flow slides: "exchanges": a question bubble, then a card holding 2 phones as the answer.
- Settings: a slash-command palette next to a phone grid.
- One dark slide: voice or live mode, with a waveform.

## Avoid

Product shelves, polaroids, heavy decoration around the bubbles.
