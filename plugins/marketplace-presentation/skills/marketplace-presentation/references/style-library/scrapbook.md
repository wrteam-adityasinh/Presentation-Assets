# Scrapbook

**Fits:** dating, social, events, kids, photo, family, community apps.
**Reference:** Bondly, folder `bondly/`, tag `bondly-v1.0.0`.

## Signature

- **Header:** an italic handwritten-style note "Chapter 03 — first impressions" in the accent; a postage-stamp index on the right (white stamp with perforated edge, dashed inner frame, "No. 03 / 15").
- **Headline:** heaviest weight; one key word in the accent with a hand-drawn loop (oval stroke, 4 px) around it. Measure the word with a trailing marker before placing the loop.
- **Devices:** polaroid prints (white frame, wide caption strip with an italic caption and a number), tilted ±2–10°, held with semi-transparent washi tape; some slides use blush-tinted iPhones.
- **Motifs:** heart (or product-symbol) stickers, "It's a match!"-style badges, clotheslines with clothespins, ticket stubs, photo-booth strips, a pinboard with pushpins, an envelope on the cover.

## Slide notes

- Cover: centred headline, an envelope with 5 polaroids fanning out of it.
- Numbers: taped note cards.
- Features: polaroid cards with captions.
- Onboarding: polaroids on 2 clotheslines.
- Profile and settings: photo-booth strips of 3 screens.
- Sheets: a pinboard.

## Avoid

Hard grids, technical labels, all caps.
