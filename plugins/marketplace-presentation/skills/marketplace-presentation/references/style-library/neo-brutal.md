# Neo-brutal retail

**Fits:** shopping, sport, gear, food delivery, anything with products and prices.
**Reference:** BicyCart, folder `bicycart/`, tag `bicycart-v1.0.1`.

## Signature

- **Header:** "CHAPTER 03 — LABEL" with a solid accent square before it. A giant outlined chapter number sits in the background, top right (stroke only, 2 px, ~400 px tall).
- **Headline:** ALL CAPS, heaviest weight, line one in ink, line two in the accent.
- **Devices:** chunky phones with a 4 px black outline and a hard offset shadow (12 px x/y, no blur, ink colour).
- **Motifs:** solid accent "stage" blocks behind phones; rotated sticker badges; accent/ink ticker bands of repeated words across the slide; spec-sheet tables with 2 px borders; product "shelves" with price-tag labels; an order receipt with dotted leaders and a barcode.

## Slide notes

- Numbers: a bordered spec sheet.
- Features: receipt lines ("ITEM / WHAT YOU GET") or sticker cards.
- Flow slides: a phone on a stage block plus 2–3 call-out lines with labels.
- Components: shelves with price-tag labels per group.
- One dark slide: cart and checkout.

## Avoid

Soft gradients, blurred shadows, rounded pill-heavy UI.
