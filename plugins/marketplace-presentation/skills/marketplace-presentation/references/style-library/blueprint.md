# Blueprint (new)

**Fits:** finance, banking, wallets, admin, SaaS, construction, real estate.
**Reference:** none yet. The first product built with it becomes the reference: add its folder and tag to `README.md`.

## Signature

- **Header:** a drawing title block on the right, like an engineering sheet: "SHEET 03 / 15 · SCALE 1:1 · REV A", in a bordered box with thin rules. On the left, a section label with a small crosshair mark.
- **Headline:** Bold, ink colour; the key word in the accent with a thin dimension line under it (line with end ticks and a small measurement label such as "24 px").
- **Devices:** frameless screens with a 1 px outline and corner registration marks (small L-shapes at each corner), as if pinned on a drawing.
- **Motifs:** a faint 24 px grid on the canvas (accent at 6% opacity); dimension lines with arrows between elements; numbered call-out circles with leader lines; spec tables with thin rules; isometric "exploded" stacks of 3 screens.

## Slide notes

- Numbers: a spec table in the title-block style.
- Features: numbered call-outs around one large screen, each with a benefit line.
- Flow slides: screens laid out like a technical drawing with dimension lines between steps.
- Design system: colour swatches with spec labels, type ramp with measured sizes.
- One dark slide: a "blueprint" version (darkest neutral or primary 900 background, white lines).

## Avoid

Stickers, tilted devices, playful copy.
