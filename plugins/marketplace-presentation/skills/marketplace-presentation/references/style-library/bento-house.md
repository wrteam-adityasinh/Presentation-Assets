# Bento house

**Fits:** services, booking, hospitality, admin dashboards. Closest to the original HotelDash house style.
**Reference:** Home Service, folder `home-service/`, tag `home-service-v1.0.3`.

## Signature

- **Header:** small numbered eyebrow in the second accent ("01 — Overview"), centred. Slide index as a quiet "01 / 15" top right.
- **Headline:** centred, two lines, two-tone: line one in ink, line two in the accent. Heaviest weight, 96–120 px.
- **Devices:** classic black-bezel phones, straight (no tilt), soft shadow.
- **Motifs:** bento grids of rounded cards (24–32 px radius) mixing a big screen card, stat cards and short feature cards; deep navy/darkest-neutral panels for contrast; small stat badges floating over screens.

## Slide notes

- Cover: centred headline, 3 phones in a fan with floating stat badges.
- Numbers: bento of 6–8 stat cards, one large.
- Features: bento grid, each card with icon, name, benefit and a screen crop.
- Flow slides: alternate "big phone + bento of 3 cards" with "row of 4–5 phones".
- Components: dark panel (allowed as the one dark slide).
- CTA: accent band with two phones.

## Avoid

Tilted devices, handwritten notes, decorative illustrations.
