# Route & battery

**Fits:** mobility, EV charging, maps, logistics, parking, fleet, ride sharing.
**Reference:** PlugNPark, folder `plugnpark/`, tag `plugnpark-v1.0.0`.

## Signature

- **Header:** a lightning-bolt (or route-pin) label on the left; a battery-shaped slide index on the right ("03 / 15" inside a battery whose fill = progress).
- **Headline:** SemiBold, the accent line has a thick "charge bar" underline in the accent.
- **Devices:** graphite (dark neutral metal) iPhones with a soft accent "charging pad" glow under them.
- **Motifs:** road and route lines (dashed centre lines) winding across slides; map pins with numbers; a top-down parking lot with stalls labelled A-01…; battery-shaped stat cards; a charge ring for live states.

## Slide notes

- Numbers: battery-shaped stat cards.
- Features: route stops: each feature is a numbered pin along a road with its benefit.
- Flow slides: phones placed along a winding road as numbered stops; a parking-lot grid for many screens.
- One dark slide: a live state (charging, tracking, navigation).

## Avoid

Handwritten notes, polaroids, serif-like editorial type.
